The Data Interaction Group is looking forward to [IEEE VIS 2026](https://www.ieeevis.org/year/2026/welcome/) in Boston, November 9–13. Our work this year spans accessible data experiences, AI agents, and interactive visualization tools. Come say hi!

## Awards

Congratulations to Frank Elavsky, Chieri J Nnadozie, Lucas Nadolskis, Patrick Carrington, and Dominik Moritz for the **Best Paper award** for [Skeleton: Visual Authoring of Non-visual Data Experiences]({{ "/publications/2026-skeleton.html" | relative_url }})!

Congratulations also to Arvind Satyanarayan, Dominik Moritz, Kanit Wongsuphasawat, and Jeffrey Heer for the **10-year InfoVis Test of Time Award** for [Vega-Lite: A Grammar of Interactive Graphics]({{ "/publications/2017-vega-lite.html" | relative_url }})! The [award](https://www.ieeevis.org/year/2026/info/awards/test-of-time-awards/) recognizes the paper presented at InfoVis 2016.

## Workshops and community

Frank is co-organizing the third [AccessViz workshop](https://accessviz.github.io/) on accessible data visualization and co-chairing Cluster, the Practitioners' Summit. Dominik is joining the panel "Industry Meets Academia: Data and Visual Analytics in the Agentic Era" and chairing the session "That's way too many dimensions for me."

See the [conference program](https://www.ieeevis.org/year/2026/program/content/) for session details and updates. We look forward to seeing everyone in Boston!
